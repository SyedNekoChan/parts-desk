    Get one free at aistudio.google.com/apikey. Stored in this browser only — never put it in the repository.
    Keep this key on the free tier. Don't attach a billing account to its Google Cloud project. A key with no billing account cannot generate a charge — when you reach the daily limit it simply stops until the quota resets, rather than billing you for the overflow.
    Everyone needs their own key. Google counts usage per project, not per key, so a shared key means a shared 500-a-day pool. One key each and you each get your own.
    Only these two include free Google Search on the free tier. The Gemini 3 models charge for search from the first query, so they aren't offered here.
    Pacing
    The free tier allows only a few requests a minute. A gap of 4–6 seconds keeps a long batch from tripping the limit. When it trips anyway, the app waits as long as Google asks and picks up where it left off.
